1. Understand what gamma means

xi and yi are liquid and vapor mole fractions. For the liquid reference used here, ai = γixi. The activity coefficient γi represents departure from ideal-solution behavior; γi = 1 is the ideal limit. Mole fractions, activities and activity coefficients are dimensionless.

At equilibrium, with ideal vapor and negligible Poynting correction:
yiP ≈ xiγiPisat, so γi ≈ yiP / (xiPisat).
Use P and Psat in the same pressure unit. Do not apply this division to an absent component at a pure endpoint.

Explain what the result establishes

7. Separate four questions

QuestionEvidence and limits
Did the model fit?Inspect training residuals, error units and warnings. Low error does not establish correct properties, consistency or prediction.
Are properties credible?Check equation, log base, signs, units and range. Compare real-fluid Psat with an independent reliable source at the same T. Hand/Lab agreement checks implementation only.
Are data consistent?Use an applicable Gibbs-Duhem-based diagnostic; report assumptions and coverage. A pass does not prove experimental accuracy. Missing endpoints and noisy derivatives limit conclusions.
Will it predict?Test observations excluded from fitting within a justified range. A single-temperature fit does not validate temperature extrapolation.

Keep Unmeasured endpoint intervals → No extrapolation · no full-area verdict. A withheld verdict reflects limited evidence; do not assume endpoints merely to obtain a verdict.

At fixed T and P, Gibbs-Duhem links the activity coefficients. Lab 08 isothermal diagnostics neglect excess-volume pressure effects. An isobaric differential verdict needs independent HE data for the temperature correction.

8. Give a claim, a number and a limitation

“For 19 synthetic points at 350 K, NRTL reduced mean absolute pressure error from 1.141% to approximately 1.14 × 10⁻⁶%. This improves training-data reproduction. Because NRTL generated the data, it does not demonstrate experimental consistency or new-temperature prediction.”

Always identify the dataset, model, settings, quantity, value and unit.

9. If results differ, check in this order

  1. Table: 19 baseline rows; correct component order; mole fractions, precision, missing cells and repeated x₁.
  2. Properties: measured units, equation forms, constants, equation units and validity limits in K. Revalidate.
  3. Fit: correct model; NRTL α = 0.3; temperature dependence off; completed fit and warnings.
  4. Evidence: correct error metric and units. Save study JSON when asking for help so the setup can be inspected.
