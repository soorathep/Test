# Week 4 student documents

The lesson replaces class while the instructor attends the ECS meeting.
All student text is US English. The worksheet has six pages, the exact-control
Lab guide four and worked solutions four. `preclass-week-04.pdf` is an optional
three-page background reading. Its scientific content is unchanged; instructions
now match independent study with no extra required preparation or follow-up.

Run from the website root with the absolute interpreter path:

```sh
~/.venvs/research/bin/python _teaching-src/2105603/prereading/week04/build_week04.py --output teaching/2105603/files
~/.venvs/research/bin/python _teaching-src/2105603/prereading/figures/preclass_figures.py
~/.venvs/research/bin/python _teaching-src/2105603/prereading/tools/build_prereading.py --week 4 --output teaching/2105603/files
```

The source imports the shared Teal-Amber Lab Palette and embedded DejaVu fonts.
All cases are synthetic and checked against independent arithmetic and the
shipped Lab engines. The known-pressure adsorption example uses 0.01400908 mol;
the Lab 11 investigation instead uses 0.010 mol. Do not conflate these cases.

Worksheet mapping: p1 A/B, p2 C, p3 D, p4 E/F, p5 G, p6 H/I. Ten work pauses total
120 minutes, plus a 10-minute break. The player reports actual recording time.
Pause predictions precede the Lab 10 demonstration (slide16) and Lab 11 (slide30).
Independent reruns follow at18/32. Attempt H before checking solutions. The
existing anonymous Session4 exit/feedback form is submitted once at39; verify
its receipt at40. No deadlines or attendance identification are invented.

The final offline ZIP must be rebuilt only after the PDFs, deck, hub and player
have been frozen. Its video links point online and preserve chapter fragments.
