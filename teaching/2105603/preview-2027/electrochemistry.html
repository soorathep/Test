Activity coefficients and model assumptions

Activities use a = γc/c°. The standard concentration c° is 1 mol/L. The supplied coefficients are assumptions for this teaching example.

Both standard reduction potentials use the same hypothetical reference and equal 0 V at 298.15 K. Two electrons transfer per reaction as written. Pure metal is present in each compartment, with activity 1. The model uses a zero liquid-junction potential and predicts reversible cell potential.

Individual-ion activities depend on the chosen convention. Bulk solutions must be electroneutral. The supplied γ values are teaching inputs.
