Keep these distinctions clear

Bridge from the opening: a consistency diagnostic, a model reproduction test and a phase-stability calculation answer different questions. A phase-equilibrium condition also does not determine how much of a phase is present; the inventory balance supplies that information.

Bulk sublimation and surface adsorption use different equilibrium descriptions. A sublimation-pressure equality applies while the solid phase remains; the finite inventory may leave only undersaturated vapor. Use volatile partial pressure in that condition, not total pressure.

In adsorption, q is absolute uptake per kg of dry adsorbent. The adsorbed amount is mass times uptake. Matching the Henry slope to Langmuir at low pressure does not make their finite-pressure predictions identical. The models do not calculate reaction equilibrium, kinetics or thermal transients.

The known-pressure example on slide 28 requires 0.01400908 mol total at 10 kPa. The Lab 11 investigation instead starts from 0.0100 mol and solves for pressure. Keep those different cases separate.
