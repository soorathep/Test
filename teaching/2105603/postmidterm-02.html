Activity and its reference

\mu_i=\mu_i^\circ+RT\ln a_i,\qquad a_i=\gamma_i x_i

The activity is dimensionless; γ is its correction relative to an ideal liquid solution using the pure-liquid standard.

For x₁=0.4 and γ₁=1.25, a₁=0.50. Residual and excess reference comparisons are in the reading and appendix.

activity ไม่มีหน่วย และ a ไม่ใช่ γ ต้องคูณด้วยองค์ประกอบ x

Properties → prediction → fitting

  1. Verify the Antoine form, units and applicable temperature range.
  2. Predict P and y from a chosen liquid model.
  3. Infer activities from observations, then compare model fits.

\gamma_i=\frac{y_iP}{x_iP_i^{sat}(T)}

This inversion assumes ideal vapor and negligible Poynting correction. Convergence, consistency and prediction are separate checks.

ตรวจรูปสมการ Antoine หน่วย และช่วงอุณหภูมิ ก่อนเชื่อค่าที่ fit ได้

A vapor-pressure error becomes an activity error

Synthetic point: P=100 kPa, x₁=0.4, y₁=0.6 and P₁ˢᵃᵗ=120 kPa.

\gamma_1=\frac{0.6(100)}{0.4(120)}=1.25

Using 150 kPa instead yields γ₁=1.00. The measured mixture data did not change; the inferred liquid behavior did.

Pause: calculate a₁ for each case before comparing answers.

ข้อมูลสารผสมเดิม แต่ใส่ความดันไอผิด ก็ทำให้แปลผล γ ผิดได้

An independent check

Work alone first, then exchange checks using the worksheet.

  1. Write the Antoine equation, temperature unit and pressure unit; evaluate one Psat.
  2. Substitute one observed T, P, x, y point into γ=yP/(xPsat); show units cancelling.
  3. Check x₁+x₂=1 and y₁+y₂=1. Compare the same observations and property convention.

Discussion · 20 minutes

Does a small fitting error mean the data and model are trustworthy?

Compare one residual pattern and one numerical check. Distinguish a converged fit, a consistency diagnostic and prediction on excluded data.
