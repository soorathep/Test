Advanced Chemical Engineering Thermodynamics

Fifteen weeks in two halves. Weeks 1–7, structure and the pure fluid: the formal machinery of thermodynamics, its molecular basis, and the equations of state built on it. Weeks 9–15, mixtures: solution thermodynamics, phase and reaction equilibrium, and what happens when a model is fitted to data rather than derived.
