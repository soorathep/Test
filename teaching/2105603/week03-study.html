What your evidence should show

  1. For Lab 03, record the dataset, sampling resolution, perturbation and amplitude. Compare the area and local diagnostics on the same settings, and inspect the residual plot.
  2. For Lab 04, keep the model and its parameter fixed while changing only the feed composition. Record the actual calculated tie-line endpoints and phase fractions, then close the material balance independently.
  3. Write one supported conclusion and one limitation for each lab. A small fit residual, thermodynamic consistency and stable phase equilibrium are different checks.

The hand-calculation endpoints 0.2 and 0.8 illustrate the lever rule. They are not the expected endpoints of the Lab 04 model. Use the endpoints the lab calculates for your chosen parameters.
