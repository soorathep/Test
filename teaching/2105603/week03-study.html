Keep the questions separate

  1. Consistency: identify the experimental path and assumptions. Interpret the area, local and modeling evidence separately. A local peak may remain visible even when an averaged criterion is met.
  2. Resolution: compare the localized defect at 41 and 161 points under the same model settings. More synthetic points change sampling and differentiation; they do not add experimental measurements.
  3. Stability: distinguish the hypothetical homogeneous feed from its final equilibrium. A positive-curvature feed can be metastable and still lower its Gibbs energy by splitting.
  4. Amounts: use the actual Lab 04 endpoints for the independent balance. Supplied hand-example or unseen-case endpoints belong to their own questions and are not expected outputs of the A = 3 model.
  5. Transfer: attempt H1–H4 on new values before checking the solutions. Keep the original reasoning and corrections.
