3 A manual feasible step

Starting at (0,0), choose p=(6,4). The trial point is (6α,4α). Capacity b=3 requires α≤0.3.

This is a manual demonstration, rather than a recorded SLSQP iteration. From (1.8,1.2), the correction (0.2,−0.2) stays on the boundary and reaches (2,1).

Correction along the active boundary

Start at (1.8,1.2), with direction d=(0.2,−0.2). The point after step t is (1.8+0.2t, 1.2−0.2t).

Along this segment, f(t)=2.08−0.16t+0.08t². Setting f′(t)=−0.16+0.16t=0 gives t=1.

4 Newton on a nonconvex function

q(s)=(s²−1)², s₀=0.3. Raw Newton uses p=−q′/q″. A descent direction requires q′p<0.

A positive directional derivative means sufficiently small positive steps ascend. Reducing the step size alone cannot fix that local direction test.

5 A minimum without a KKT multiplier

Minimize f(x)=x subject to g(x)=x²≤0. Only x=0 is feasible. Stationarity would require 1+2λx=0 with λ≥0.


CheckValue

At x=0, ∇g=0 and the stationarity residual is 1 for every λ. The point is a minimum because no other feasible point exists. A suitable constraint qualification is missing.
