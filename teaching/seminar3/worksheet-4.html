4A. Audit a battery paragraph

Synthetic classroom evidence, not published experimental data: one LFP/graphite cell model at 25 °C; three independent cells per protocol; a common diagnostic at cycle 200.

Measurement Protocol A Protocol B
Mean capacity retention at cycle 200 88% 94%

Individual cell values, uncertainty estimates, lifetime endpoints and plating diagnostics are not supplied.

Instructor-written mock edit: “B extends battery lifetime by 6%, eliminates lithium plating, and works under all conditions [REF-X].” REF-X is an invented placeholder, not a real citation.

For each claim, give a decision and an evidence-based reason. Then write a supported replacement sentence. The video includes a 60-second interval; pause longer if needed.

4B. Confidentiality decision

A partner sends unpublished cycling data under a project agreement. A colleague removes the company name and proposes uploading the file to an AI service. Is that sufficient? Give the immediate next action and a workable alternative while permission is unresolved.

4C. Plan your own verification

Choose a nonconfidential paragraph about your research, or use the synthetic battery example. You do not need to upload anything. Write the task, what must stay fixed, the independent check, and what you would record. State one change you would reject.
