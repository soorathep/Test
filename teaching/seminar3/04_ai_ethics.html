A fluent edit changes the evidence

01

Supplied: mean retention was 88% for A and 94% for B at cycle 200.

02

Mock edit: “B extends battery lifetime by 6%, eliminates lithium plating, and works under all conditions [REF-X].”

03

REF-X is an invented classroom placeholder.

Audit answer: retain the measured outcome

Claim Decision and reason
Longer lifetime Revise: retention at cycle 200 was measured.
Eliminates plating Remove: no plating diagnostic supplied.
All conditions Restrict: one cell model at 25 °C.
REF-X Remove: invented, unverified reference.

The number and its denominator

01

94% − 88% = 6 percentage points in mean retention.

02

(94 − 88) / 88 × 100 ≈ 6.8% relative to A.

03

Neither calculation measures a lifetime extension.

Arithmetic from synthetic teaching values; no inference about statistical significance.

A defensible revised paragraph

01

At cycle 200 and 25 °C, mean retention was 88% for A and 94% for B in the tested LFP/graphite cell model.

02

The observed difference was 6 percentage points, with 3 independent cells per protocol.

03

No uncertainty estimate or plating diagnostic is supplied.

A reference needs two checks

01

Existence: locate the original publication and verify its details.

02

Support: read the passage or result relevant to your claim.

03

A real DOI does not prove that the citation supports the sentence.

A bounded editing request

01

Improve clarity while preserving values, units and scope.

02

Add no mechanism, reference or factual claim.

03

List changes that could alter meaning; compare the result yourself.
