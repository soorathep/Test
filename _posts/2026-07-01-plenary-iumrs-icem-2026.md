Assoc. Prof. Dr. Soorathep Kheawhom delivered a **plenary lecture** at **IUMRS-ICEM 2026** — the 19th
International Conference on Electronic Materials — held **28 June – 1 July 2026** at **The Empress
Convention Centre, Chiang Mai, Thailand**.

![Plenary lecture at IUMRS-ICEM 2026, Chiang Mai]({{ site.baseurl }}/img/news/plenary-iumrs-icem-2026.jpg)

The talk, **"Beyond Materials: Understanding Electrochemical Energy Systems through Operando Science,"**
argued that durability in non-lithium batteries is produced by a causal chain rather than by any single
material property. Read through the group's **Coordination–Transport–Interface (CTI)** framework — from
the first solvation shell, through bulk transport, to interfacial evolution — and watched with operando
synchrotron characterization, the electrochemical system, not the material alone, becomes the unit of
study.

Our thanks to the organisers of IUMRS-ICEM 2026 for the invitation.
