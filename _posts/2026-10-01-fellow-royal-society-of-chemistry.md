The original paper certificate has finally arrived. **Assoc. Prof. Soorathep Kheawhom** has been admitted as a **Fellow of the Royal Society of Chemistry (FRSC)**, with effect from **2 September 2026**.

![Certificate of admission as a Fellow of the Royal Society of Chemistry, Soorathep Kheawhom, 2 September 2026]({{ site.baseurl }}/img/news/frsc-certificate-2026.webp)

All of this is the shared work of the Energy Storage Innovation Laboratory at Chulalongkorn University, on non-lithium batteries, organic flow batteries and operando synchrotron characterisation. Our sincere thanks go to the students, postdoctoral researchers, collaborators and referees who made it possible.

Fellowship is a senior grade of membership of the Royal Society of Chemistry, awarded on assessment of a sustained contribution to the chemical sciences.
