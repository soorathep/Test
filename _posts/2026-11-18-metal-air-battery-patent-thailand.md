Thai Patent No. **125525**, *Metal-air battery system*, was granted by the Department of Intellectual Property on **6 October 2026**. The patent is co-owned by **Mitsubishi Heavy Industries, Ltd.** and **Chulalongkorn University** and comes from our zinc-air battery collaboration with MHI.

![Patent certificates for the metal-air battery system: EP4343930 (Europe), JP7733377 (Japan), AU2022301848 (Australia) and TH125525 (Thailand)]({{ site.baseurl }}/img/news/patents-metal-air-2026.webp)

The cell uses three concentric tubular electrodes, with separate electrodes for charging and discharging, and a circulating electrolyte for energy storage. Placing the electrodes that need lower resistance on the larger outer rings reduces their current density and makes charging and discharging more efficient.

The patent family (WO2023276778) is now granted in four jurisdictions:

- Europe: EP4343930, granted 27 August 2025
- Japan: JP7733377, granted 26 August 2025
- Australia: AU2022301848, granted 8 May 2025
- Thailand: TH125525, granted 6 October 2026

The US application (18/563,378) is under examination. Our thanks go to the MHI team and the members of the Energy Storage Innovation Laboratory.
