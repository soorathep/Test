Thai Patent No. **125525**, *Metal-air battery system*, was granted by the Department of Intellectual Property on **6 October 2026**. The patent is co-owned by **Mitsubishi Heavy Industries, Ltd.** and **Chulalongkorn University** and comes from our zinc-air battery collaboration with MHI. The corresponding **US Patent No. 12,803,085 B2** was issued on **17 November 2026**.

![Patent certificates for the metal-air battery system: EP4343930 (Europe), JP7733377 (Japan), AU2022301848 (Australia), TH125525 (Thailand) and US12803085 B2 (United States)]({{ site.baseurl }}/img/news/patents-metal-air-2026.webp)

The cell uses three concentric tubular electrodes, with separate electrodes for charging and discharging, and a circulating electrolyte for energy storage. Placing the electrodes that need lower resistance on the larger outer rings reduces their current density and makes charging and discharging more efficient.

The patent family (WO2023276778) is now granted in five jurisdictions:

- Europe: EP4343930, granted 27 August 2025
- Japan: JP7733377, granted 26 August 2025
- Australia: AU2022301848, granted 8 May 2025
- Thailand: TH125525, granted 6 October 2026
- United States: US12803085 B2, issued 17 November 2026

Our thanks go to the MHI team and the members of the Energy Storage Innovation Laboratory.
